\noindent{\bf Fields of Expertise}

\medskip

Representations of finite and infinite dimensional Lie algebras,
generalized Harish-Chandra modules, representations of  Lie
superalgebras and Lie supergroups, geometry of supermanifolds,
homogeneous superspaces, super $D$-modules, homogeneous ind-spaces
and geometry of ind-varieties.
