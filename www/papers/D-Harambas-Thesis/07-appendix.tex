This appendix displays the minimal injective resolutions of the $\mathfrak{sp}_\infty-$modules $\Gamma_{<\lambda>},$ with $|\lambda|\leq 12$. Any resolution
$$0\to\Gamma_{<\lambda>}\tof{\alpha_0}\Gamma_{\lambda} = \Gamma_\lambda^0\tof{\alpha_1} \Gamma_\lambda^1\tof{\alpha_2} ...\tof{\alpha_{s}}\Gamma_\lambda^s \to 0$$ is formatted in a vertical setting, with the bottom row being $\Gamma^0_\lambda = \Gamma_\lambda,$ the next row being $\Gamma^1_\lambda,$ and so on up to the top row, which is the last term of a minimal injective resolution of $\Gamma_{<\lambda>}$: 
\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{c}
     $\Gamma_\lambda^s$ \\ \hline
     $\Gamma_\lambda^{s-1}$ \\ \hline
     \vdots \\ \hline
     $\Gamma_\lambda^1$ \\ \hline
     $\Gamma_\lambda^0 = \Gamma_\lambda$
\end{tabular}\hspace{0.2cm}.
\end{table}
