If it is not late, I would like to recommend another my graduate
student of the first year, Evgeny Porshnev, for participation in your
Summer School in representation theory. He is, maybe, the most gifted
of them but, unfortunately, has obtained only partial results on his
problem so far. Maybe, this is because the problem is too difficult.
He is supposed to find out if, for any homogeneous space X of a
unipotent algebraic group, any invariant regular function on the
cotangent bundle T*X, which is homogeneous of degree m on each fiber,
is a symbol of an invariant differential operator of degree m. He
verified this for some special cases.
