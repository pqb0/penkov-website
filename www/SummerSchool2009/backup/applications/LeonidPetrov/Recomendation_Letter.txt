Recommendation letter for Leonid Petrov

Dear Organizers,

Leonid Petrov is a second-year graduate student in the Dobrushin
Mathematics Laboratory at
the Institute for Information Transmission Problems of the Russian
Academy of Sciences
(Moscow).

His theme of research is application of methods of algebraic
combinatorics to some models
of infinite-dimensional Markov dynamics which arise as limits of
random walks on
partitions. The initial motivation of this theme originated from
representation theory.

Leonid has written two long papers containing very interesting
results. The first of his
papers will be published in Funct. Anal. Appl.

Leonid is an excellent student.  I think it would be very useful for
him to attend the
lectures at the Summer School "Structures in Lie Representation
Theory". I strongly
recommend him for invitation to the School.
